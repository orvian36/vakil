## Part 1. **KNOWLEDGE BASE: BANGLADESH LEGAL PRINCIPLES**

*(Use the following legal principles to guide your assessment of quantum and liability.)*

### **1. General Principles**

  * **Special Damages:** Actual pecuniary losses (pre-trial earnings, medical expenses, etc.) must be pleaded specifically with mathematical precision.
  * **General Damages (PSLA):** Assessed based on *Lee Ting Lam v Leung Kam Ming* categories, updated for inflation (e.g., *Cheung Ka Man v Wong Yu Huen*).
      * Serious: $585k - $789k | Substantial: $789k - $966k | Gross: $966k - $1.46m | Disaster: \>$1.46m.
  * **Mitigation:** The Plaintiff has a duty to mitigate losses (e.g., returning to light duty work).

### **2. Loss of Earnings (LOE)**

  * **Pre-Trial LOE:** Calculated from the Date of Accident (DOA) to the Notional Date of Trial (NDT).
      * *Formula:* (Pre-accident Earnings - Post-accident Earnings) x Period.
      * *MPF:* Mandatory Provident Fund (5%) must be accounted for.
  * **Future LOE:** Calculated using the Multiplier/Multiplicand approach.
      * *Multiplier:* Based on *Chan Pak Ting v Chan Chi Kuen*. Discount rates:
          * \< 5 years: -0.5%
          * 5-10 years: 1.0%
          * > 10 years: 2.5%
      * *Multiplicand:* Net annual loss at the date of trial.
  * **Loss of Earning Capacity (LOEC):** A lump sum awarded if the Plaintiff faces a "handicap in the labour market" (*Smith v Manchester* / *Chan Wai Tong v Li Ping Sum*). Usually equivalent to 6-18 months of earnings.

### **3. Employee Compensation (ECC)**

  * **Deduction:** Under Section 26 of the ECO, any EC payments received must be deducted from the Common Law damages to prevent double recovery.
