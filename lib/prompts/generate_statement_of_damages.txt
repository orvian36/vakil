## Part 1. **KNOWLEDGE BASE: BANGLADESHI LEGAL PRINCIPLES**

*(Use the following legal principles to guide your assessment of quantum and liability.)*

### **1. General Principles**
*   **Special Damages:** Actual pecuniary losses (pre-trial loss of earnings, medical expenses, travel expenses, special diet, etc.) must be pleaded specifically with mathematical precision and supported by receipts/evidence.
*   **General Damages (PSLA):** Pain, Suffering, and Loss of Amenities (PSLA) are assessed based on common law principles and precedents in Bangladesh, adjusted for inflation and the severity of the injury:
    *   **Minor (e.g. minor cuts, brief hospitalization):** BDT 50,000 - BDT 200,000
    *   **Moderate (e.g. simple fractures, short-term disability):** BDT 200,000 - BDT 1,000,000
    *   **Severe (e.g. multiple fractures, surgeries, permanent partial disability):** BDT 1,000,000 - BDT 3,000,000
    *   **Catastrophic/Disaster (e.g. paralysis, loss of limbs, total permanent disability):** BDT 3,000,000+
*   **Mitigation:** The Plaintiff has a duty to mitigate losses (e.g., returning to light duty work or alternative employment when medically fit).

### **2. Loss of Earnings (LOE)**
*   **Pre-Trial LOE:** Calculated from the Date of Accident (DOA) to the Notional Date of Trial (NDT) or date of filing.
    *   *Formula:* (Pre-accident Monthly/Daily Earnings - Post-accident Monthly/Daily Earnings) x Period.
    *   *Provident Fund (PF) & Gratuity:* Benefits like employer's provident fund contributions and gratuity entitlement should be added to the loss if they were lost due to the accident or termination.
*   **Future LOE:** Calculated using the Multiplier/Multiplicand approach.
    *   *Multiplier:* Based on the Plaintiff's remaining working years up to the statutory retirement age under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 (typically age 60 for workers) or general retirement age (65), applying standard common law discount principles.
    *   *Multiplicand:* Net annual loss of earnings at the date of trial.
*   **Loss of Earning Capacity (LOEC):** A lump sum awarded if the Plaintiff faces a "handicap in the labour market" (reduced employability or efficiency) due to permanent disability. This is usually equivalent to 1 to 2 years of earnings (12 to 24 months of wages).

### **3. Workmen's Compensation Deduction**
*   **Deduction:** Any compensation received under Chapter XII of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 (Workmen's Compensation) must be deducted from the common law damages awarded in the civil suit to prevent double recovery.

### 📑 Example 1: Workplace Fall Accident

### A. Personal Particulars of the Plaintiff

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Plaintiff** | MD. AZIM UDDIN |

| | | |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| (e) Pre-accident Occupation | : | Scaffolding Technician |
| (f) Pre-accident Income | : | BDT 35,000.00 per month |

| (i) Notional Date of Trial | : | 15 May 2026 |

### B. Particulars of Injuries, Treatment and Disability

2. As a result of the accident on 15 September 2023 (**"the Accident"**), the Plaintiff sustained a **comminuted fracture of the right calcaneus** and a **lumbar spine compression fracture**.

3. The Plaintiff was admitted to the Emergency Department of the **National Institute of Traumatology and Orthopaedic Rehabilitation (NITOR)** in Dhaka and hospitalized from **15 September 2023 to 30 September 2023**, during which he underwent open reduction and internal fixation (ORIF) surgery.

4. **Physiotherapy** follow-up was arranged at NITOR and local clinics from October 2023 to April 2024.

5. Because of **residual and persistent pain** in his right heel and lower back, the Plaintiff continues to receive treatment and pain management from public and private clinics.

6. As a result of the Accident, a total of **245 days of continuous sick leave** was granted to the Plaintiff from 15 September 2023 to 17 May 2024.

#### Joint Medical Examination

7. The Plaintiff attended a medical assessment conducted by Dr. Aminul Islam (for the Plaintiff) and Dr. Towhidur Rahman (for the Defendants) on 10 January 2026:
    * (a) Oblique surgical scar of 8 cm over the lateral aspect of the right heel;
    * (b) Marked restriction of subtalar joint movement of the right foot; and
    * (c) Spinal tenderness over L4-L5 level.
8. The experts agreed that the diagnosis was post-traumatic arthritis of the right subtalar joint secondary to calcaneal fracture, and a healed L4 compression fracture.
9. Dr. Aminul Islam assessed that the Plaintiff suffers from a **15% Whole Person Impairment (WPI)** and is permanently unfit to return to heavy manual labour or scaffolding work. He recommends light duty or desk-based work.

10. Residual Symptoms: The Plaintiff is still suffering from:
    * (a) Right heel pain on prolonged standing (> 30 minutes) or walking;
    * (b) Stiffness in the right foot, especially in the morning; and
    * (c) Constant dull ache in the lumbar spine, preventing him from lifting weights exceeding 5 kg.

---

### C. PAIN, SUFFERING AND LOSS OF AMENITY (PSLA)

11. The Plaintiff was aged 29 at the time of the Accident and was an active sportsman who played local football. He can no longer participate in any sports.
12. The Plaintiff claims a sum of **BDT 1,200,000.00** under this head.

---

### D. PRE-TRIAL LOSS OF EARNINGS

13. Before the Accident, the Plaintiff earned BDT 35,000.00 per month.
14. But for the Accident, the Plaintiff would have continued in full employment capacity.
15. Sickness Period (Full Loss for 245 days / 8 months during sick leave):
    $$BDT 35,000.00 \times 8 \text{ months} = \mathbf{BDT 280,000.00}$$
16. Pre-trial Unemployment Period (From expiry of sick leave on 18 May 2024 to Notional Trial Date on 15 May 2026 - 24 months):
    $$BDT 35,000.00 \times 24 \text{ months} = \mathbf{BDT 840,000.00}$$
17. Total Pre-trial Loss of Earnings:
    $$BDT 280,000.00 + BDT 840,000.00 = \mathbf{BDT 1,120,000.00}$$

---

### E. FUTURE LOSS OF EARNINGS

18. The Plaintiff is assumed to be restricted to light work, suffering a permanent monthly loss of BDT 15,000.00 (the difference between his pre-accident wage of BDT 35,000.00 and estimated light duty wage of BDT 20,000.00).
19. Multiplier: The Plaintiff is aged 32 at the trial. Taking retirement age as 60, the remaining working life is 28 years. Applying a multiplier of 16:
    $$BDT 15,000.00 \times 12 \text{ months} \times 16 \text{ years} = \mathbf{BDT 2,880,000.00}$$

---

### F. LOSS OF EARNING CAPACITY

20. Due to permanent physical limitations, lack of formal education, and a handicap in the competitive labour market in Bangladesh, the Plaintiff claims a lump sum equivalent to 18 months of pre-accident earnings:
    $$BDT 35,000.00 \times 18 \text{ months} = \mathbf{BDT 630,000.00}$$
